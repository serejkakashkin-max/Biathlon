# Provisional ski physics v1

Authoritative baseline: Assets/Resources/Biathlon/ski_physics_provisional_v1.json. All coefficients are
provisional mechanical model inputs; no final race-time or equipment balance claim. Mass includes the
effective moving athlete/equipment mass. Delivered physiological watts are the available mechanical
propulsion input before future technique/equipment modifiers. Maximum speed is a numerical envelope,
far above expected racing speeds; it must not silently become the cornering or athlete skill model.

WP-017 includes baseline snow friction/drag and exposed signed wind. Dynamic snow, technique, tuck,
cornering, equipment and drafting remain their named subsequent WPs. ADR-0004 specifies integration.
