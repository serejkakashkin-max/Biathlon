# Provisional tuck contract

TuckDefinition JSON controls negative grade/minimum speed eligibility and skill-interpolated
absolute curvature/risk limits. Rejected requests preserve original effort and upright drag/control.
Eligible tuck caps effective effort before PhysiologySession, multiplies drag area in SkiForceSystem
and exposes a reduced steering-response multiplier for the later steering adapter.
It never increases a low/coasting effort request. HR/recovery changes arise only through M1 workload.
Apply technique after this reduced physiological output; never keep full requested propulsion while
charging the reduced workload. At low speed or on a steep climb the aerodynamic benefit is unavailable.

Pure evaluation emits no events itself: authoritative runner owns posture transitions and will
record changes through IRaceEventSink alongside effective input, rejection and drag telemetry.
No positional steering or animation authority is claimed here. All skill thresholds and balance
values are provisional; cornering enforces additional speed/line constraints in WP020.
