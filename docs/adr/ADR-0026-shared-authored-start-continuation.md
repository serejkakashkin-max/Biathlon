# ADR0026 — shared authored start continuation

2026-10-02. TASK169J; bounded WP169 prerequisite. Extends ADR0016/0024/0025. Start-continuation proof and AI start traffic are implemented and tested; complete production race acceptance remains pending.

The authored Mass Start approach joins Main at its zero endpoint. An exclusive junction guard cannot admit a parallel grid there, but dropping the guard would lose protection against terminal Main traffic and other branches. A cold grid check alone also cannot prove safe bodies through curved approach knots or establish runtime liveness.

An optional versioned `PhysicalStartContinuationDefinition` captures the exact track, approach, junction, directed connection and occupancy snapshots, authoritative planar world polylines, and world validation policy. Its open source corridor covers the complete approach; its outgoing corridor covers an explicit Main prefix. Captured knots use whole-millimetre logical coordinates. The source endpoint and Main zero are a directed world-continuous seam. The production binding captures the 40m approach and first 10m of Main. The null-option legacy branch retains its previous context and policy.

`PhysicalStartContinuationMap` proves separation of complete swept body envelopes over each common time interval. A body is the configured planar rectangle: half the longitudinal separation and half the lateral separation, including the configured personal space. On a straight polyline edge the centre and lateral offset are affine, so projection endpoints enclose the whole sweep. At a heading knot, all four corners, including the lateral centre offset, rotate through the shortest signed heading arc. Each axis projection is `origin + A*cos(theta) + B*sin(theta)`; arc endpoints and enclosed maximum/minimum critical angles give its complete angular support. Both incident tangents remain represented. An exactly antiparallel heading has two possible shortest arcs and retains a conservative radial envelope. Missing separation or unknown geometry remains blocking.

This proof assumes the captured planar polyline and rectangular body model are authoritative, lateral movement is affine within the receipt interval, and knot orientation follows the shortest signed arc. It does not prove separation of an arbitrary rendered mesh, an unbound world spline, or another branch. It uses all captured corridor edge axes plus world axes. A single axis separating the complete envelopes is sufficient; the absence of such an axis conservatively retains conflict. The outward seam-policy margin, transformed one-nanometre receipt reserve and IEEE arithmetic reserve remain in force. Zero-forward lateral moves include both lateral endpoints and their knot rotations.

Only a first-lap source/Main-prefix conflict belonging to this exact continuation may use the world proof in place of its one shared exclusive start guard. Terminal Main traffic, other ports, other junctions and traffic outside the captured prefix retain their physical guards. Incoming source body footprints and their lateral locks survive until their accepted paid travel clears them. A front-row athlete with zero remaining approach distance still owns the physical incoming source endpoint until the field accepts the handoff; zero remaining distance is not an admission grant.

The initial full-radial knot envelope blocked safe, separated authored rows. Exact angular corner support resolves that false rejection, while close asynchronous rows can still be physically or conservatively blocked. The AI must therefore avoid creating those close pass/draft rows during the start. A separate typed, versioned, data-driven `AiStartTrafficPolicyDefinition` holds each AI athlete's authored grid lateral position and suppresses AI overtakes while its actual physical point belongs to the source loop, including the front endpoint. It is loaded and recorded only for an explicitly shared start continuation. The field still pays lateral work, limits movement and resolves every body/guard. The player-command branch precedes this policy and preserves player steering. Shooting geometry, effort capability, body margins and difficulty multipliers are unchanged.

The verified production prefix clears all 30 athletes from the source by tick1000. Unit and integration coverage includes knot corner support, narrow blocked pairs, zero-forward lateral sweeps, scaled nanometre reserves, incoming endpoint ownership, complete fixture replay, paid player steering and legacy isolation. Debug/Release prefix1000 agrees exactly for all actor coordinates and events; changing build optimization does not change capability or tuning.

This decision establishes a start prerequisite. The immutable production 120000-tick run remains `PREFIX_ONLY`: it clears the start but stalls at the first narrow range paths while AI retains broad ski lines. Physical-range approach/exit steering and dense queue behaviour are TASK169N. A complete repeated production race, deterministic event/replay comparison and required Unity/visual acceptance are still needed before WP169 or M13 can be declared complete.
