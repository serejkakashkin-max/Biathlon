# FROSTLINE athlete creator — first implementation

Subject: a winter endurance athlete preparing a career. The screen's job is to
let the player recognize the person they are creating and understand their starting
trade-offs before committing a profile.

Retain the approved FROSTLINE palette: ink #E8F5F8, deep #0B1928, surface #132A3B,
glacier #85D5DF, gold #D6AA69 and muted #B0C4D0. Use local Oswald for sport display
figures/headings and Roboto for controls and ability data, with the existing OFL
credits. OVR is deliberately prominent because the owner makes it a central metric;
it must be labelled separately from readiness, points and rank.

Composition: left biography/editing steps; central live, rotatable G2 athlete;
right permanent ability and remaining specialization budget. The memorable element
is the actual athlete changing in place, carrying the approved motion and national
kit into the real race. Keep decorative chrome quiet rather than adding a new visual
theme. No generated mockup substitutes for the model or acceptance captures.

Identity / look / skills are real editing steps. Confirmation is a separate durable
boundary. Group ratings remain readable on constrained4:3 at130% text size;13
allocation controls paginate4 at a time. View buttons provide keyboard/gamepad
alternatives to mouse orbit/zoom. Reduced motion freezes the skate preview and face
editing remains still. Existing focus/error/contrast behaviour is retained.

Native evidence: ../../quality/m15-6. The current screen is an implemented initial
own-athlete path, not acceptance of all M15.6 appearance or career requirements.
