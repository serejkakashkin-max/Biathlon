## Coverage and scope

`dotnet/Biathlon.ReliabilityLab` drives the real `FrontEndSession`, training
previews, confirmed pre-race checkpoints, actual shared production race authority,
result collection/publication and file-store replacement. It creates a fresh
Continue session after every week. The player uses the same authored training
planner as its AI peers for this controlled reliability experiment. All entrants
are driven by the ordinary AI controller during each race; this does not claim
three human-played seasons or48 native-rendered races.

Each real race retains its complete authority result payload/hash, seed, field,
geometric shot count, tick count and event hash. Every weekly boundary retains
the previous/next save digest, world/archive counts and actual save size. The
study also exercises cancel/reprepare, abandon/retry once per season, failed
training/result writes, duplicate-result rejection, previous-generation backup
retention, exact pre-race source checkpoints, source identity/cosmetic/talent
stability and final corrupt-primary recovery/repair.

Separate native `CareerProfileReliabilityTests` cover real pre-race interaction,
five real saved training weeks, checkpoint failure preserving the prepared race,
same-family backup/checkpoint recovery and disk faults. The old50-year generation
test and15 annual transition test remain valuable boundary fixtures; they do not
substitute for this full weekly/race soak and are not counted as48 actual races.

Mass Start of season2 exceeded the normal200000-tick budget. The exact career
and race seed were retained. An independent replay of that prepared state found
one stationary weak rival at main179.458m on a10% uphill after four bouts;29
opponents finished. It requested effort613 indefinitely. It was neither blocked
by another body nor exhausting a course-contact response budget.

The minimized force witness evaluates the actual athlete's physiology,35/37
ski/climbing technique, recorded fatigue/reserve and grade. The ordinary pace
cannot generate positive breakaway acceleration, while ordinary full effort can
and consumes real anaerobic reserve. An optional authored AI restart controller
now requests that same ordinary effort after a sustained uphill stall, with
reserve recovery and speed/resource hysteresis. Manual player input, range
movement and ordinary braking safety remain intact. Physics, ability ratings,
resource rules, shooting and race tick budgets are unchanged. ADR-0072 records
this explicitly authorized reliability correction and its material AI impact.
