## Delivered

- Accepted presentation events drive rifle, bolt, target hit/miss and cheering.
  Audio neither chooses outcomes nor advances authority. Shooting heartbeat uses
  the actual simulation beat count, exposed through a read-only snapshot property.
- Three ski-snow layers vary with snow, speed and load; pole variants stop in tuck.
  Breathing responds to effort/heart rate, held breath suppresses breathing while
  heartbeat remains audible. Nearby opponents' rifle reports are spatial.
- Wind, snowfall, stadium crowd, clean-bout/finish cheer, menu ambience and interface
  cues use 24 original offline-generated clips. Source provenance and hashes ship
  in `AUDIO_SOURCES.json`; reproduction script is `tools/audio/generate_m15_audio.py`.
- Five native AudioMixer buses, six reserved loops and sixteen transient sources.
  No per-shot object creation or runtime synthesis; loaded PCM uses 16,925,760 bytes.
  Accepted event requests are capped at twelve per rendered frame, prioritizing the
  observed athlete and dropping overflow instead of queuing a future burst.
- Pause, pursuit skipping, fast finish, seek and accelerated replay suppress race
  audio. Normal-speed replay and live spectator cameras update listener context.
- RU/EN volume controls: master, effects, body, ambience, interface and menu bed;
  soft mix, mute when unfocused and rifle preview. Preferences are separately
  versioned; corrupt values fall back. Career schema and graphics are unchanged.

Main files: `BiathlonAudio.cs`, `BiathlonAudioSettings.cs`, `RaceAudioPresentation.cs`,
`FrontEndAudioSettings.cs`, host/feed/replay/spectator adapters, `M15AudioAuthoring.cs`,
`PlayerBuildM15.cs`, audio Resources and focused tests. See ADR-0067 for constraints.

## Limits and next step

The soundbank is original stylized sound design, not field recordings. Numerical
checks do not establish subjective realism, comfort or owner approval. Next step
is the owner's headphone/speaker playtest: skiing on different snow, a crowded
range, breath hold, pause/replay, soft mix and volume persistence. M16 is the next
planned milestone after this review; it has not been started automatically.

Pre-existing unrelated local changes were preserved and excluded from M15 commits.
The build uses the existing working content baseline plus these M15 sources; the
delivery does not claim that unrelated dirty resources were reverted to HEAD.
