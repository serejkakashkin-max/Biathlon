## Review checklist for every M11 screenshot

When comparing a Unity capture to references, check:

- Is the athlete silhouette readable?
- Does the pose clearly communicate the locomotion/shooting state?
- Are skis/poles/rifle attached coherently?
- Are there obvious penetrations/floating contacts?
- Is the player readable at the primary chase-camera distance?
- Do nearby rivals remain readable?
- Does the result preserve authoritative gameplay?
- Does it avoid accidental copied branding?
