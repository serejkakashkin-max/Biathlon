# Production range — official geometric reference review

2026-10-03. Owner directive: [maximum field and realistic range](../../owner/OWNER_M13_RANGE_REALISM_FIELD_CAP_2026-10-03.md). WP-169 remains IN_PROGRESS.

The original official documents were downloaded for read-only research outside Unity Assets. Three diagrams were actually rendered and inspected: Lenzerheide PDF pages 11 and 12, and Osrblie PDF page 2. Local source hashes and rendered-page hashes are retained in `artifacts/wp169-realism/official-plans-source-binding.json`. No diagram, logo or branded venue content was imported into the game.

The [Lenzerheide 2025 media guide](https://www.biathlon-lenzerheide.ch/microsites-assets/biathlon-lenzerheide/pdf/medien/media-guide-lenzerheide-2025-en.pdf) shows a compact rectangular range, lane numbers 30 at the left and 1 at the right, a penalty loop beside the right side of the range, and separate start/transit/finish areas with spectators behind them. Its format diagrams preserve this relation for Sprint, Pursuit and Mass Start while changing start staging. The following course-map pages are available for discipline-specific loop research; no claim of reproducing the real courses is made.

The [Osrblie 2019 detailed stadium plan](https://osrblie2019.biathlon.sk/public/site/files/source/Mapy/Stadion/Plan_of_stadium_final.pdf) explicitly dimensions the competitor ramp at 11.10 m behind the firing line. Arrows run left to right through that ramp toward the right-hand 150 m penalty loop and onward course. Coaches, media and VIP strips lie behind the ramp; finish traffic and stands have separate space farther back. This is a useful original-layout reference for shared range circulation instead of thirty widely separated miniature course loops.

The [2026 IBU rules](https://assets.ctfassets.net/cz0vl36hcq0x/7mkQn1kQTB7VzlUZHAcuec/c8897efcf14ccded0f82af3513c6e5ef/ECR_Rules_2026_cap03.pdf), sections 3.4.2–3.4.4, set the measurable baseline: left entry/right exit, 2.75–3 m lane widths, 2 × 1.5 m mats of 1–2 cm thickness, a 10–12 m rear ramp, 30 target banks and approximately 50 m firing distance. The current 6 m pitch and reversed circulation fail this intended production baseline.

Engineering interpretation: build an original 30-lane stadium with a coherent rear ramp and side circulation, distinct spectator/staff/finish areas and a right-hand penalty flow. Keep mat presentation attached to the actual authoritative stops. Realistic lane pitch does not justify reducing athlete collision envelopes or silently ignoring path intersections. The new path design must prove complete-body clearance and count-distance fairness before import. White rectangles remain a separate unconfirmed rendering diagnosis.

Foundation resume regression: 14/14 PASS, zero failures/skips, canonical integer hash retained; TRX `artifacts/wp169-realism/healthy-baseline/foundation-resume.trx`. This healthy baseline is not acceptance of the new range or 40-athlete field.
