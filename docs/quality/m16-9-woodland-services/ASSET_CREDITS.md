# Woodland Basin service areas

- [Dark Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_planks), Rob Tuytel. The timber material uses the 1K diffuse texture.
- [CheeseBox_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/CheeseBox_01), Gabriel Radić. The open base and diffuse map are reused as service equipment crates. The mesh was converted from the published glTF to OBJ and placed at several scales.

Both assets are released under [Poly Haven's CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license), which permits redistribution and commercial use. The tents, lodge, ski stands, skyline shader, and spectator meshes are original project assets.
